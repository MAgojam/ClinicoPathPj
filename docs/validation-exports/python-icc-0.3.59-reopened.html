Balanced ICC assumes independent cases and the selected rater design. For two-way models every complete case is rated by the same selected raters; one-way columns represent balanced measurement slots. Single-rater reliability and reliability of the mean of k ratings are different inferential units. Normal, homoscedastic ANOVA errors underpin intervals. Case-ID checks identify known repeated IDs but cannot prove independence or the sampling design. No clinical interpretation bands, automatic outlier removal or imputation. CCC, categorical agreement, unbalanced/repeated/hierarchical models and bootstrap inference are not implemented. Bland-Altman output is available by changing the analysis method; an ICC does not replace a differences-based agreement assessment.
