Correlation inspector

Local inspection only; native options determine the calculation. Select a matrix cell to inspect n, exclusions, intervals and multiplicity-adjusted evidence. Gray cells are unavailable, not zero. Diagonal cells are self-correlations and are not tested. Tab enters or leaves the matrix. Arrow keys move between cells; Home/End move within a row; Ctrl/Command+Home/End go to the first/last cell. Enter/Space inspect a cell.
